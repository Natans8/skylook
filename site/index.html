Open the viewer

Every sky in World of Warcraft 9.2.7, the client Epsilon runs, pictured through the day. Open one to watch its whole day turn, or copy a command to put it in the game.

An effect is drawn over the view, so each one is pictured over black, two seconds in.

The pictures are drawn from the game's own files, and the viewer fetches those files from wago.tools when a sky is opened. World of Warcraft is a trademark of Blizzard Entertainment, and Skylook is a fan project not affiliated with it. Source
